# STAR Framework

## (S) - Situation
What was the context/background for the situation you were in? Where did it occur, when did it happen? 
What was the goal?

## (T) - Task
What was your role?
What were you trying to achieve? Why was it important?
What were the risks/consequences if nothing happened?

## (A) - Action
What did you personally own?
How did you do it? How did you influence the outcome? Who else was involved?
What was the most significant obstacle you faced?

## (R) - Result
How did you measure success for this project? What results did you achieve? (e.g., metrics)
What trade-offs did you have to make to achieve this? What did you learn or would have done differently?
